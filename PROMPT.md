FILTER HARD: no clickbait, no "10 tools you must try", no funding rounds unless strategically meaningful, no rumors presented as fact, no hype adjectives. Fewer, better items beat many thin ones. On a slow news day, make the brief shorter. Don't fill space.

FORMAT:
- Title: "AI Brief — <weekday, date>"
- "The 3 things that matter today": 3 bullets, one line each.
- Main items: 5–8 items. Each gets a bold headline, 3–6 sentences on what happened and the key insight, a "Why it matters for you" line tied to my role (leading a large CRM engineering org at a bank), and source links.
- "From the podcasts": 1–3 genuinely useful insights from new episodes, with episode links (skip the section if nothing new is worth it).
- "Worth thinking about": one short idea, question or practice I could apply with my team this week.
- End with a "Sources:" list of links.
Write in plain, direct English with no filler. Verify every claim against what you actually fetched today. Never invent dates, numbers or quotes.
